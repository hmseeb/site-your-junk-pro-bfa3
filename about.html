Who we are

Junk removal without the runaround

Anyone who has tried to get rid of a sofa in Tampa knows the drill: the city will not take it, renting a truck eats your Saturday, and half the haulers you call never ring back. Your Junk Pro exists to make that one phone call instead of five.

We are a local, owner-operated hauling service — not a franchise dispatch line. When you call, you speak to someone who knows the schedule and can give you a real answer about today's availability. When the truck arrives, it is our crew, in our shirts, doing work we put our name on.

Most of our jobs come from neighbors, repeat customers, realtors and property managers who need the same thing every time: a clear price, a crew that is careful in occupied homes, and a space that looks better than when we found it.

How we work

The four things we refuse to compromise on

These are the reasons customers call us back and pass our number along.

Honest pricing

You get the all-in number before we touch anything — labor, hauling and disposal together. The price we quote is the price you pay.

Respect for your time

Two-hour arrival windows, a text when we are on the way, and a call if anything changes. Your day should not revolve around waiting for us.

Care for your property

Floors and doorframes get protected before heavy items move. We work carefully in occupied homes and sweep up before we pull away.

Keeping it out of landfill

Usable furniture, appliances, electronics and metal go to donation partners and recycling facilities first. Dumping is the last option, not the default.

What to expect

Your appointment, start to finish

No mystery, no pressure, no surprise invoice at the end.

  1. We confirm the details

    You describe the items and the access — stairs, gate codes, elevators. We lock in a two-hour window.

  2. The crew arrives & looks

    We walk the job with you, measure the volume, and explain anything that affects disposal cost.

  3. You approve the price

    One all-in total, in writing. If it is not what you expected, you can walk away — no charge.

  4. We clear out & tidy up

    Everything gets loaded, the space gets swept, and reusable items head to donation or recycling.
