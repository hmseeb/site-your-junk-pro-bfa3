Disposal & coverage

Where it goes and where we go

Landfill is our last resort, not our default. Items in usable condition — furniture, working appliances, housewares, tools — are routed to local donation partners. Metal, appliances, electronics, cardboard and yard waste go to the appropriate recycling and processing facilities.

Only what genuinely cannot be reused or recycled is taken to a licensed disposal site.
