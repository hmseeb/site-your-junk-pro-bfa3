How it works

Four easy steps from clutter to clear

No dumpster permits, no renting a truck, no weekend lost to the landfill.

  1. Tell us what you have

    Call, text or send the quote form. A photo of the pile is the fastest way to get a price.

  2. Lock in your window

    Pick a time that works — including same-day and next-day slots when our schedule allows.

  3. Approve the price on site

    We look at the volume, quote you the all-in total, and only start once you say go.

  4. We load, sweep and go

    Everything gets carried out, the area gets tidied, and usable items head to donation or recycling.

For safety and legal reasons we cannot haul hazardous materials. That generally includes:

  • Paint, solvents, oil, fuel and chemicals
  • Asbestos and other regulated materials
  • Medical or biohazardous waste
  • Propane tanks and ammunition

If you are unsure about something, call and ask — we will tell you straight away and can usually point you to the right disposal site.

Landfill is our last resort. Furniture, appliances, electronics, metal and household goods in usable condition are routed to donation centers and recycling facilities whenever possible. Only what truly cannot be reused or recycled is disposed of.
