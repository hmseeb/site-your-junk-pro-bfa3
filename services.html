Appliance & furniture removal

Appliances are heavy, sharp-cornered and usually in the worst possible spot. We bring dollies, straps and enough hands to get them out without damaging your doorframes.

Metal appliances are taken to scrap and recycling facilities rather than the landfill, and working units in good condition are offered to donation partners first.

Garage, attic & shed cleanouts

The garage you cannot park in, the attic you dread opening, the shed slowly losing its roof. We clear them out in one visit and take apart anything that needs to come down.

  • Garages & carports — tools, paint-free leftovers, bikes, lawn equipment
  • Attics & crawl spaces — boxes, insulation scraps, old storage
  • Sheds & outbuildings — contents removed, structure dismantled if needed
  • Closets & spare rooms — shelving and built-ins removed
  • Hot tubs, playsets & trampolines — disassembled and hauled

Teardown included
